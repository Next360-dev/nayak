## 3. Source of truth: what really exists
Auth/session: `useAuth()` -> `{ user, loading, signOut(pushToken) }`; `User = { username, role: 'admin'|'mla'|'minister'|'staff', scoped, constituency_name?, politician_name?, district_name?, leader_title?, leader_party?, can_share?, must_change_password? }`. `login(u,p)`, `changePassword(current,new)` in `src/api/client.ts`. 429 = locked out ("Too many failed attempts. Try again in 15 minutes.").
News (`src/api/news.ts`): `fetchNews({period, news_scope: all|positive|negative|statewide|civic, page, limit, constituency, date_range, specific_date}) -> {articles, social_posts?, social_stats?{total_posts,positive_posts,negative_posts}, scope_counts?, pagination?{page,limit,total,pages,has_next}, counts?}`; `fetchSocialMedia({constituency, mla_name, platform, sentiment, period, search, limit, offset})`; `startRefresh()` POST /api/news/refresh; `fetchRefreshStatus()`; `translate(headline, summary)`. `PAGE_SIZE=20` in `src/constants/config.ts`. Article fields: id, headline, summary, source, url, date_time, priority_score (1 = urgent), sentiment (positive|negative|other), district_name, constituency_name, image_url (usually null: do not build a photo UI around it).
Shares (`src/api/shares.ts`): `fetchInbox()->Share[]`, `fetchSent()->SentShare[]`, `fetchUnreadCount()`, `updateStatus(id,'seen'|'acting'|'done')`, `fetchReplies(id, recipient?)`, `postReply(id, body, recipient?)` (max `MAX_REPLY_CHARS`=2000), `fetchTargets()->{username,role,name?}[]`, `createShare({title,url,summary,note,priority:'normal'|'urgent',recipients[]})`. `Share={id,sender,title,url,summary,note,priority,status,created_at,reply_count}`; `SentShare` adds `recipients:{recipient,status}[]`. Rules: staff share only to their own leader (preselected); leader only to own staff; admin never shares (hide all share UI for admin).
Team board (`src/api/board.ts`): `fetchBoard()->{leader, can_manage, me, members[], tasks[]}`; `TeamTask={id,title,note,url,created_by,assigned_to,due_date,status:'todo'|'doing'|'done',notes?}`; `createTask({title,note,url,assigned_to,due_date})`, `updateTask(id,{status,assigned_to,due_date})`, `fetchTaskNotes(id)`, `addTaskNote(id,body)`, `fetchTeamBriefing(lang)`, `saveTeamBriefing(lang,body)`, `resetTeamBriefing(lang)`. Leaders read tasks and write notes; staff (`can_manage`) create/move.
My team (`board.ts`): `fetchTeam()->{username,last_login,is_active}[]`, `createTeamMember(username,password)`, `resetTeamPassword(username,pw)`, `setTeamActive(username,active)`. Own staff only.
Admin users: `fetchAllUsers()->AdminUser[]` (`username,role,politician_name,constituency_name,district_name,works_for,last_login,is_active`), `resetUserPassword`, `setUserActive` (admin accounts cannot be disabled).
Passwords you set from the app must be >= 12 characters (server enforces; show the rule).
Dashboard: `fetchDashboard()` (Home is DONE, do not redo).
Existing routes: `app/(auth)/login`, `app/(tabs)/{index,news,inbox,sent,team,users,settings}`, `app/share/create`, `app/share/[id]`, `app/my-team`, `app/change-password`. Tab bar shows (by role) leader: Home, News, Inbox, Team, More; staff: same; admin: Home, Inbox, Users, More. `sent` route stays but is hidden from the bar. Existing query keys: ['news'], ['social',...], ['inbox'], ['sent'], ['board'], ['my-team'], ['all-users'], ['targets'], ['replies', id], ['dashboard'], ['unread-count']. Keep them (other screens invalidate them).
